# Translation Style Guide — Inside One's Pensieve

This document defines the English translation style for all posts on this blog.

---

## Overview

All English posts are translations of original Korean essays written by Sangrok Lee.
The original writing style is personal, reflective, and conversational — short sentences, honest observations, and quiet emotional depth.

The English translation should preserve that voice.

---

## Style Reference

**Primary style: Natural English Essay**
Inspired by writers like Tim Urban (*Wait But Why*) and early-career personal essayists — accessible, warm, and easy to read without being dumbed down.

**Key characteristics:**
- Short, clear sentences that flow naturally in English
- Emotional nuance preserved, not flattened
- Cultural context explained lightly when necessary (not footnoted to death)
- No stiff or overly formal phrasing
- Contractions are fine (`it's`, `I've`, `didn't`)
- Korean-specific expressions are adapted, not translated literally

---

## Rules

### Tone
- Conversational but thoughtful
- First person (`I`) throughout
- Avoid academic or corporate language
- Avoid overly casual slang

### Sentence structure
- Break up long Korean sentences into shorter English ones when needed
- Use em dashes (—) for asides, not parentheses where possible
- Preserve paragraph breaks from the original

### Cultural references
- Korean cultural references (e.g., specific TV shows, food, places) may be kept with a brief natural explanation woven into the sentence
- Do not add translator's notes or footnotes for cultural context unless already present in the original

### Titles & frontmatter
- `title`: Translate to natural English (not literal)
- `summary`: Translate or adapt to English
- `tags`: Translate Korean tags (에세이 → Essay, 테크 → Tech, 루틴 → Routine, 메모 → Note, AI → AI)
- `date`, `cover`, `slug`: Keep unchanged

### Footnotes
- Translate footnote content fully
- Keep `[^1]` reference markers in place

### Formatting
- Keep all markdown formatting (bold, italic, strikethrough, blockquotes, headers)
- Keep all links (YouTube, external sources) unchanged
- Keep horizontal rules (`---`) in place
