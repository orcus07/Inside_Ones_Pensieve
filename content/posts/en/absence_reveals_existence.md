Someone once said the easiest way to know how you feel about a person is to say goodbye to them right away. (*Shoe Dog*)

When something is there, it feels natural. But when it is missing just when it ought to be there, we look for it and miss it all the more. Why did I not say thank you, that I was glad, that I was happy to see them, more often while they were beside me?

In legal terms, failing to do what one ought to do is called an ‘omission.’ If a person who has a duty to act does not act, they have broken the law.

That is why I want to be sincere with the people around me today. It is what I feel I ought to do. On an uneven day like today, I draw a line of focus and keep it unbroken. I want my heart to follow that line and flow out in one go, without getting caught in the thoughts in my head. Each day, I arrange and imagine the thread of context I have let flow branching into two, then becoming tributaries and rivers, and finally the sea.

On the day I fail to do that, I think I would accept it if everyone unanimously called me guilty of omission.

I open the door, hoping we live well even through the days that change us little by little. If we are not pushed along but move forward of our own accord, that is enough for me. Until we find each other again someday, after all the winding paths.

See u when I see u
