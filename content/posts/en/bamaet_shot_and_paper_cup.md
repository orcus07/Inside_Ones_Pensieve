As you get older, your talk improves and you can speak for longer stretches. But the concentration of good words actually thins out. Why is that?
It's because the daily sediment builds up and your thinking hardens. Time isn't a blessing for everyone. If you let time pass without trying to understand, random thoughts pile up in your head. If you don't sort and organize them carefully, raw ideas tangle and grow until your thinking becomes rigid. ~~(You can’t outsource your understanding)~~

Rigid thinking itself becomes long runs of speech. When those people hit a trigger word, they unload a preloaded stream of breathy talk. It’s like a trainee at SM Entertainment—one of those K-pop trainees—who's practiced so much the play button hits even in their sleep. The harder they try to have a conversation, the longer their monologue gets—and the farther it drifts from the context. It's a vicious loop.
Even if my thinking capacity is as small and insignificant as a paper cup, there's a way to keep it clear. Pour without overflowing your "shamelessness" threshold, and drink with a boy's eagerness—only as thirsty as you are. If you diligently empty and pour this way, even something small and fragile won't get soaked. You can keep drinking clean water for a long time. Worrying about what to fill it with before how much to fill it with seems like a better way to live fully.
If you only fill and never empty, all kinds of liquor and syrup residue collect into a bar-mat shot ( [Bar Mat Shot](https://www.mashed.com/1773119/bartenders-hate-bar-mat-shot/))[^1]. It's free booze nobody wants.
Don't just fill—shake it out and empty it. Focus and align the frameworks you think in.
Don't dump your thoughts everywhere—be someone who pours without overflowing.

---

[^1]: A shot made from the liquid collected on the rubber mat behind the bar. Known by various names — Route 66, Jersey Turnpike, Buffalo Sweat, Matt Damon. Anything the bartender handled that night — beer, wine, spilled cocktails, pickle juice, cleaning solution — can end up in this free shot.
