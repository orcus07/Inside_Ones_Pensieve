Looking back, there have been days when my life changed completely—and yet, on those days, I never really felt it at the moment.

That’s because when something amazing happens, I’m so quick to jump to the “right” reaction that I hardly have time to actually feel it.

Once the version of me that performs for others quiets down, I quietly check in on my real feelings.

What am I feeling right now?

Am I about to jump for joy? Am I stunned? Or just calm?

What’s for sure is that something unbelievable—something that makes no sense—happened today.

I’m not shocked because I’ve believed it would happen someday. But I honestly can’t imagine what’s coming next.

I started building a happy home.
