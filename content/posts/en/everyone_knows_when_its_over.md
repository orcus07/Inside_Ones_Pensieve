---
title: "Everyone Knows When It's Over in Hindsight"
date: 2025-11-07
summary: "Hindsight is always 20/20, but moving forward is the real challenge."
tags: [Essay]
---

Looking back or observing your surroundings is easy. Moving forward—that's the hard part.

If everything seems appealing, it's likely because you don't truly know what you want.

Consistently put yourself on the timeline every day, thinking and writing, connecting with your inner self.

Sharpen your tastes and essence, focus on your own desires, not those of others.

In a world where AI does everything for us, what if everything is better than me?

Enjoying what you accomplish with your own hands—that's what it means to be a Human Being.

We taste a diverse flavour of everything
