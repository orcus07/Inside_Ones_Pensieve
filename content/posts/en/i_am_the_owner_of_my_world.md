Film critic Lee Dong-jin's rating of the movie 'Hope' has become a hot topic. (I highly recommend reading Lee Dong-jin's blog post about it.)

This reminded me of a movie I saw a while ago. The protagonist of the movie 'Free Guy,' Guy, is an NPC in the GTA game. Every day, he wakes up at 9 AM, puts on a blue shirt and khakis, greets his goldfish, grabs a medium coffee with one cream and two sugars from his favorite cafe, goes to work at the bank, gets robbed, and ends his day. Then, one day, his life changes when he orders a 'cappuccino'—something he'd never tried before. In GTA, to level up, you usually have to defeat others and steal weapons. But Guy becomes famous by helping people and doing good deeds instead.

![](/images/i_am_the_owner_of_my_world_img2.jpg)

We all live as protagonists within our own unique universes and contexts. However, in someone else's world, I might be a supporting character, or perhaps an NPC who just needs to stick to the background. But when I, the owner of my world, say 'Hope' isn't fun, some mere NPC—who should be in the background—says they disagree with me. The narrative of my life, where I'm the protagonist, was shattered by an NPC.

Why would my life be shattered by a single word from an NPC? Maybe it's because I'm living a life that just flows along, like an NPC. If someone else's words can destroy my universe, then it's not my life anymore; it's a borrowed one. Does it really matter if many people flock somewhere, or if a famous critic says a movie I found boring is actually good? Why not try a cappuccino you don't usually drink, or do a small good deed for someone around you? It's important to discover your own tastes and deeply feel and move in that direction. Life is too short and busy to care what anyone else says; just go your own way.

Finally, to borrow Lee Dong-jin's words, "An opinion is not a fact, and even if many opinions lean towards a specific one, that doesn't make it a fact at all." Each person is the protagonist of their own life.

---
P.S: The protagonist of the movie 'The Owner of the World' is a high school girl named 'Juin' (meaning 'owner'). I won't spoil it, but the movie is about the world surrounding Juin and the world she creates. It might seem like your life will be ruined if you live at odds with everyone, but even if you say what you want and live how you want, life doesn't fall apart that easily. These days, I want to be the kind of person who waves out the window.
