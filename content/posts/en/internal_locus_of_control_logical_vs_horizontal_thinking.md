---
title: "Internal Locus of Control: Logical vs. Horizontal Thinking"
date: 2025-09-17
summary: "Which is your Locus of Control?"
tags: [Essay]
---

At the beginning of the year, I had my Saju read. It wasn't the kind involving spirit possession or shamanic rituals; instead, it was a method of predicting the future by combining the client's three-character name and birth date.

The Saju reader said they'd taught children as a math teacher for a long time. They found it fascinating that some children excelled exactly as much as they were taught, while others didn't improve no matter how much they were taught. This made them realize that everyone has their own tendencies and destiny, which led them to study Saju. They also said that Saju isn't about telling the future, but about giving clients conviction.

From the perspective of social psychology's Locus of Control[^1], **internalists** believe that individuals can control their own lives. They think that if they put in effort, the results will be successful. Conversely, **externalists** believe that external factors control individuals. They feel that luck or chance plays a significant role in their lives.

The Saju reading told me that I have a lot of "metal" in my Saju, indicating strong innate tendencies and dispositions. It also said that while I was born with a scientific and engineering inclination, I also possess literary qualities, which always leads me to try different things.

Listening to the Saju interpretation, I thought that the inertia in my life might be quite strong. Looking back, my job placements and career changes were more heavily influenced by the paths I'd already walked. But even if everything is predetermined, even if it's my destiny to turn a huge wheel, I think I'll always wonder if *I* want to turn it. Isn't my life about me creating the opportunities and me living it?
