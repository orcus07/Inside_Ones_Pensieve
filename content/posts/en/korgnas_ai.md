### Task vs. Purpose
[](https://substack.com/@sangroklee)
I've structured Jensen Huang's interview in original/translated quote format. The conclusions can probably be summarized into two points.
1. **AI will reduce tasks and allow us to focus on purpose when we work.**
> A job has tasks and has purpose
> 
> A job has **tasks** and **purpose**.
> 
> 
> And in the case of a radiologist, the task is to study scans, but the purpose is to diagnose disease, and to research, and and that exactly, and they’re doing research
> 
> For example, a radiologist's **task** is to study scans, but their **purpose** is to diagnose and research diseases.
> 
> 
> My task, but my purpose is obviously not typing
> 
> My **task** is typing, but my **purpose** is clearly not typing.
> 
> 
> If Nvidia was more productive, it doesn’t result in layoffs, it results in us doing more, more things
> 
> If Nvidia becomes more productive, it doesn't lead to layoffs—it leads to us **doing more, more things**.
* * *
2. **Winning in AI means dominating competitors in Energy/Chips/Infrastructure/Models/Applications, and without energy, there is no AI. ~~(Long live America, long live Trump)~~**
> the easiest way to think about that is it’s kind of like a five-year, five-year, five-layer cake
> 
> The easiest way to think about it is that it's like a **five-layer cake**.
> 
> 
> When we think about, uh, energy, it"s a helpful framework because we need factories. Factories need energy, and without energy, we have no factory. Without factories, we have no AI
> 
> "When we think about energy, it's a helpful framework because we need factories. Factories need energy, and without energy, we have no factory. Without factories, we have no AI."
