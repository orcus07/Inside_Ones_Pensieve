AWS has announced that it will integrate Nvidia's NVLink Fusion technology into its own AI chip, Trainium4. This is surprising because it essentially means Amazon is replacing its proprietary network solution, the NIC System.
* The NIC (Network Interface Card) system was a core technology that allowed Amazon to become a cloud powerhouse. It enhances performance, reduces latency, and strengthens security by offloading networking tasks from the main CPU, freeing up computing and memory resources for customer workloads.

The traditional cloud ecosystem revolved around multi-tenancy for individual servers—in other words, infrastructure sharing. Users expected powerful scalability by renting anything from a single CPU core to an entire server. Hyperscalers built their own custom NICs and CPUs for this very purpose. At that time, Amazon became a dominant force in the cloud market, leveraging its overwhelming virtualization and block storage infrastructure.

However, we're now in the AI era, which uses GPU servers. **Time slicing** for individual GPU servers is virtually impossible, and individual users can't really split and use these servers. This ultimately leads to a **significant shift in the business paradigm**, affecting things like data center construction, funding, and cloud operating software.

In Christensen's theory of disruptive innovation, incumbent companies often fall into the Innovator's Dilemma, failing to adapt to the next paradigm. But just as Google abandoned its search moat and aggressively raced to the forefront of the AI era, it makes you wonder if Amazon can also transform its existing cloud moat (SaaS) into AI infrastructure (IaaS).
