Recently, an internal recording of Jensen Huang from an Nvidia all-hands meeting was leaked. In it, he reportedly told managers who were advising their teams to reduce AI usage, " _**Are you insane?**_ "

"My understanding is Nvidia has some managers who are telling their people **to use less AI. Are you insane?** I want every task that is possible to be automated with AI to be automated with AI. **I promise you; you will have work to do.** " - Jensen Huang -

It's not just Nvidia. Companies like Meta, Google, Microsoft, and Amazon are also not just encouraging, but practically forcing their employees to use AI. Even if there's a task that AI can't perform yet, they're told not to do it manually. Instead, they should keep using AI until it can handle the task.

Now, even training or optimizing AI is too slow for humans. In other words, it's a strategy to maximize productivity by entrusting even the use of AI to build AI— **"To use AI to build AI"** —to AI itself.

Task automation was once every office worker's dream. However, in reality, the productivity of office workers is now destined to be outsourced to AI. While the amount of work might not decrease, it's become abundantly clear that office workers with lower productivity than AI will lose their competitiveness.
