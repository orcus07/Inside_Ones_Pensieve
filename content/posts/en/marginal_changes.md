1. I decided to only play YouTube videos that offer something to learn.
2. I ended up unsubscribing from or organizing channels that were just entertaining but lacked substance.
3. Even if there's resistance, I always click on good videos.
4. I keep them playing in the background, whether I'm actively listening or not.
5. After a few days, the algorithm changed and started recommending educational videos.
6. Even with videos playing in the background, the act of watching them no longer feels trivial, so I don't click on YouTube as often.
7. Then I have more time, and if I just sit idly, I naturally start writing the essays I hadn't gotten around to, or I read a book.
8. And when I get bored again, I repeat from step 1.

The key lies in that split-second decision to put down your phone when you're sitting at your desk or lying on the sofa, scrolling through Facebook after a long day.
