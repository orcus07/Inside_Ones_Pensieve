1. Every day, I turn my work PC and phone off and on again. Electronic devices get a bit faster after an initial reset through their power sequence. Seeing the OS login screen makes me feel like I'm starting work, which gives me a good initial reset too.
2. I watched Kian84 doing a charcoal drawing with Chungjae in 'I Live Alone'. It was refreshing and pleasant to see them naturally immerse themselves, moving their hands without much thought, emptying their accumulated thoughts, and filling themselves with a beginner's mindset.
3. Yoga is about aligning your physical self with your thoughts in reality. Dwelling on past joys or imagined future happiness can bring worries and anxiety. Hearing my partner share their thoughts made me want to go back to yoga. Lately, while preparing for a competition, my running has felt more like accumulating training mileage than emptying my mind.
4. I'm busy thinking and trying to express my thoughts. I try to absorb, digest, and then put them out there. More often than not, it doesn't go well. Sometimes they come out undercooked, other times overcooked. Just when I think it's right, I'm back to square one. A fair amount of time passes too. But it's comfortable. I'm happy that writing on Substack is steadily becoming my new habit. The length of my thoughts, once short, is gradually increasing. I'll borrow the most impressive quote from last week's book club: "It's a process of filling the unconscious. I have to believe."
