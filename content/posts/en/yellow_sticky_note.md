I came across Sam Altman's note-taking video. He told the host he likes a spring-bound notebook that fits in his pocket. That way he can tear off pages one by one, spread them around, and think more freely. It struck me — the person leading cutting-edge tech and helping open the AI era still writes and organizes thoughts in an analog way. I found that impressive.
So I chose yellow Post-its. They fit in my pocket, I can pull one out and jot things down anytime, and I can stick them wherever I want.
I made filling a Post-it the routine to start my day. The goal is to be fully alone with my thoughts — a thought experiment — and then output them in some form, whether writing or audio.
Substack will become the place where I share those accumulated scraps of thought — unsolicited letters. I don't know where they'll go or how they'll flow. But I hope that by consistently taking time to share them, they'll at least lead to good conversations.
